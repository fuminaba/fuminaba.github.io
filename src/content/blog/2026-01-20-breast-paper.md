**Description:** Co-authored a paper titled ***Large-scale DNA organization analysis of primary breast cancers for prediction of axillary lymph node metastases***, published in the journal [*The Breast*](https://www.thebreastonline.com/article/S0960-9776(26)00022-6/fulltext).

We analyzed large-scale nuclear DNA distribution patterns (chromatin organization) in primary breast cancer slides. By quantifying these nuclear features across thousands of cells, we developed an algorithm that predicts axillary lymph node metastasis, helping reduce overtreatment and refine patient staging.
